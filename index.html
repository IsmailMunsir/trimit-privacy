Privacy Policy for TrimIt

Last updated: 6 September 2026

TrimIt is a personal finance app for tracking wallets, income, expenses and subscriptions. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

In short: your financial data stays on your phone. TrimIt has no user accounts of its own, no cloud sync and no analytics. The app only contacts the internet to fetch currency exchange rates, and to sign you in with Google if you choose that option.

1. Information stored on your device

Everything you enter into TrimIt is written to a database stored locally on your phone. This includes:

  • Wallets, including names, types, currencies, balances, budgets and spending limits
  • Income and expense transactions, including amounts, categories, dates and notes
  • Transfers between your own wallets
  • Subscriptions, including names, costs, billing cycles and renewal dates
  • Payment history generated when a subscription renews
  • Savings goals and their progress
  • App settings such as your chosen theme, display currency and notification preferences

This information is not transmitted to us or to any third party. We do not operate a server that receives it, and we cannot see it.

2. Information that leaves your device

Exchange rates

To convert between currencies, TrimIt requests current exchange rates from a public rates API. This request contains no personal information and no details about your wallets or spending — it simply asks for current rates. Rates are cached on your device so conversion continues to work offline.

Google Sign-In (optional)

TrimIt offers Google Sign-In as one way to create a profile in the app. If you choose to use it, Google shares your name and email address with the app so a profile can be created. This is handled by Firebase Authentication, operated by Google.

Using Google Sign-In is entirely optional. You can create a local account with an email and password instead, or use the app without signing in at all. Passwords for local accounts are stored only as a one-way SHA-256 hash and are never recoverable in plain text.

Google's own handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not collect or transmit your financial data
  • We do not use analytics or tracking software
  • We do not show advertisements
  • We do not sell or share any information with third parties
  • We do not access your contacts, location, camera, photos or microphone

4. Permissions the app requests

PermissionWhy it is needed
Internet To fetch live currency exchange rates, and for Google Sign-In if used
Notifications To remind you before a subscription renews. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device
Exact alarms To ensure renewal reminders arrive at the intended time
Biometrics Only if you enable fingerprint or face unlock for the app's PIN lock
Boot completed To restore scheduled reminders after your phone restarts

5. Backups you create

TrimIt lets you export a backup of your data as a JSON file. This file is written to your device's storage. It is not uploaded anywhere automatically.

If you choose to share that file — by email, cloud storage or any other method — it leaves your device at that point, and its privacy then depends on the service you shared it through. The backup file is not encrypted, so treat it as you would any document containing your financial information.

6. Your control over your data

  • Export: Settings → Backup & Restore → Export backup
  • Delete everything: Settings → Backup & Restore → Clear all data
  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes its local database and all data within it
  • Google Sign-In: you can sign out at any time from the Profile screen

Because your data is stored only on your device, deleting it there deletes it completely. There is no copy held elsewhere for us to remove.

7. Children

TrimIt is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Since the app does not transmit personal data to us, no such information reaches us in any case.

8. Security

Network requests made by the app use HTTPS. You can optionally protect the app with a PIN, stored only as a one-way hash, with biometric unlock available on supported devices.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Data held on your device is also subject to your device's own security — keeping your phone locked and up to date matters.
